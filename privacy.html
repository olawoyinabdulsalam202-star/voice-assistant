Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KAIROS ("the Service," "we," "our") — an AI voice assistant developed by Elite Dev, Lagos, Nigeria — handles information when you use it. KAIROS uses both your device and our hosted services.

01Information We Process

Depending on which features you use, KAIROS may process the following:

  • Voice input — audio you speak to the system for transcription and response generation.
  • Camera images — frames captured when you use the Camera Vision feature to ask about something you show the system.
  • Screen content — screenshots or screen streams shared during Screen Sharing sessions.
  • Text input — messages typed while using Text Mode.
  • Conversation context — recent exchanges kept in short-term memory to support coherent follow-ups.
  • Reminder data — any reminders or scheduled alerts you create.
  • Location data — approximate location, only when you request weather information.
  • Account and usage data — your name, email address, subscription status, feature usage, and security events.

02How We Use This Information

Information you provide is used solely to operate the features you invoke — generating spoken or written responses, interpreting what the camera or screen shows, setting reminders, or retrieving weather data. KAIROS does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or resale to third parties.

03Storage and Retention

KAIROS stores account details, conversations, memories, reminders, subscription records, and usage counters on our hosted database so your account can work across sessions.

Some preferences and authentication details are also stored in your browser. We retain information while your account is active and as needed for security, payment records, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. You may request account deletion through the contact link.

04Third-Party Services

To generate responses, analyze images, deliver email, process payments, or fetch weather data, KAIROS sends relevant information to service providers such as AI model providers, Brevo, Paystack, and weather services. These providers process data under their own privacy terms. KAIROS uses server-managed service credentials; users are not asked to provide personal AI API keys.

05Camera and Screen Data

Images and screen content are only captured when you actively enable Camera Vision or Screen Sharing. This data is transmitted to the configured AI provider for analysis and is not separately stored or archived by KAIROS beyond the active session, unless you have configured your own logging.

06Children's Privacy

KAIROS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children in that age group.

07Your Choices

  • You can disable the camera, screen sharing, or reminders at any time.
  • You can manage stored memories and conversations from the Service.
  • You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account data through the contact link.

08Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as KAIROS evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

09Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Elite Dev, Lagos, Nigeria, via the contact link on the KAIROS homepage.
